Caveats & data-quality notes

Period covered: Sept 2017 (first month Lauren Way appears in Cindy's books) through Dec 2020. Months earlier than Sept 2017 are not in the source data.

Stale templates ⚠: Three blocks of months in the source spreadsheets appear to have copy-pasted Total cells that were never refreshed:

  • March / April / May 2018 — identical Rent ($1,554.76) and Expense ($1,203.83) all three months.
  • April through December 2020 — every month shows the same hardcoded Total Debits / Credits / Net ($352.37 / $0 / -$352.37), even though the line items differ each month. This is a templating artifact in the source spreadsheet, not a parser bug — verified against the raw cells in the original workbooks.

"Profit" definition: Rent (rental income credits) − every other debit on the Lauren Way tab (mortgage, HOA, utilities, repairs, etc.). It is NOT Net Operating Income — mortgage payments are included as expenses. To see NOI specifically, use the full dashboard and toggle Mortgage off in the filter bar.

Every row is auditable: the Drive ↗ link opens Cindy's original Google Sheet for that month. The XLSX link downloads the same file from this repo.
